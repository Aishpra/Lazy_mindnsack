Questions before you hand it over

Reasonable things to ask.

What exactly does Lazy handle?+

Depending on the chosen scope: WordPress delivery and care, technical and local SEO, content, Google Ads, conversion work, tracking and reporting.

Can you work with my existing website?+

Yes. The Website Audit & Indexing service is designed as a low-risk starting point for an existing site.

Does the Google Ads price include ad spend?+

No. The fee covers campaign management. Your media budget is paid separately and remains visible to you.

Do you guarantee rankings or leads?+

No responsible provider can guarantee a specific ranking or revenue result. We define measurable work and report the evidence.

Can I change plans later?+

Yes. You can change scope at the end of a billing cycle after completing the stated minimum term.

Will my team be able to edit the website?+

Yes. MindSnack builds with WordPress, Blocksy and Elementor Free so routine edits do not require custom development.
